# E-Leave Approver Guide

Last verified: 2026-07-23

Guide for **supervisors** and **managers** who endorse leave applications before HR.

## Who Can Approve

You see **For Approval** when your HR profile marks you as a supervisor and/or manager of employees who report through you.

If you do not see the menu item, you are not flagged as supervisor/manager for this system.

## For Approval Queue

Open **For Approval** to list applications awaiting your decision.

Typical flow:

1. Employee submits leave.
2. **Supervisor** records a decision first.
3. **Manager** records a decision second.
4. When both have approved, the request moves to the HR Approval queue.

If you are both supervisor and manager for an employee, the system may allow you to complete both endorsement steps according to backend rules.

## Making a Decision

1. Open an application from the queue.
2. Review dates, leave type, reason, address, and supporting documents.
3. Approve or disapprove (with remarks when required by the UI).

Your decision is stored on the application’s approver fields. Disapproval stops the request from reaching HR endorsement.

## What You Are Not Responsible For

- Final day-by-day HR status (with pay / without pay / cancel) — that is **HR Approval**.
- Editing leave balances or beginning balances — that is **HR admin**.

## Tips

- Confirm the employee’s dates do not conflict with known unit schedules before approving.
- If documents are missing for a leave type that requires them, disapprove or ask the employee to refile with attachments (process may vary by unit).
