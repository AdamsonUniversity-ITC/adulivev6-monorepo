# E-Leave User Guide

Last verified: 2026-07-23

Guide for employees filing and tracking their own leave in the E-Leave app.

## Getting Started

1. Open the E-Leave site (your organization URL) while signed in to AdULive.
2. Use the sidebar: **Guidelines**, **My Leave**, and other items you are allowed to see.

If you open a page you cannot access, you are redirected to **Forbidden**.

## Guidelines

**Guidelines** explains campus leave policies and filing expectations. Read this before filing unfamiliar leave types.

## My Leave

**My Leave** shows:

- Your **leave balances** (credits and pending filed leave by type)
- Your **leave applications** and their overall status

### Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Pending | Waiting on supervisor, manager, and/or HR |
| Approved | Fully approved for the application |
| Partially Approved | Some days/portions approved, others not |
| Disapproved | Rejected in the approval chain |
| Cancelled | Cancelled |

## Apply for Leave

From My Leave, choose **Apply for Leave**. Complete four steps:

1. **Dates** — Start and end dates. You may exclude Saturdays and/or Sundays from the day list.
2. **Leave type & day portions** — Choose the leave type and set Whole Day / AM / PM / Evening for each date (Evening only if your position allows it).
3. **Details** — Reason, address while on leave, optional supporting documents.
4. **Review** — Confirm and submit.

### What may block submit

- Leave type not available for your employment profile or gender (for example Paternity is male-only; Maternity is female-only).
- Filing too early/late for that leave type’s rules.
- Not enough **Paternity** credits for the days you selected (Maternity is not blocked for credits at apply time).
- A day portion that overlaps an existing leave filing on the same date (for example you already filed AM; you may still file PM that day, but not another AM or Whole Day).
- Emergency Leave with a dependent-care reason if you already used that reason twice this year.

Errors appear as a **toast** and, when possible, on the related form field. You may be taken back to the step that needs fixing.

## After You Submit

1. Your supervisor is notified to endorse the request.
2. After the supervisor approves, your manager endorses.
3. After both approve, HR processes each leave day (with or without pay, etc.).
4. Track progress under **My Leave**. Open a request to see day-level details and attachments.

### Cancel or edit while still Pending

You can **Cancel request** or **Edit** from My Leave (table Actions or the request detail) only while Approver 1, Approver 2, and HR are still **Pending**.

- Cancel is immediate. An optional reason may be entered.
- Once anyone Approves, Disapproves, or Cancels a step, Cancel and Edit are hidden.
- Editing the form is shown only for Pending requests; saving an update is not available yet.

## Tips

- Check balances on My Leave before filing multi-day leave.
- Keep supporting documents ready for types that usually need proof.
- If a toast says you already have leave on a date, check whether you already used that **portion** (AM/PM/Evening/Whole Day) on that calendar day.
